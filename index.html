Selected images versus complete studies

Benchmarks for medical imaging agents should test searching the full study, operating the viewer and producing checkable evidence.

Many benchmarks start from pre-selected slices, crops, or patches. Real imaging workflows require searching full studies, operating the viewer software, comparing views or time points, and documenting evidence that can be audited.

Conventional image question answering

  • Pre-selected diagnostically relevant 2D views.
  • Final answers only, with little record of how they were reached.
  • No record of viewer state, coordinates or where the evidence came from.

MedOpenClaw runtime

  • Complete studies in 3D Slicer and QuPath.
  • Predefined viewer actions, recorded as replayable traces together with the viewer state and generated outputs.
  • Structured evidence that can be scored against hidden masks, annotations, and labels.

A runtime for inspecting full studies and capturing evidence.

MedOpenClaw sits between a vision-language model (VLM) and standard medical imaging viewers. Agents use predefined viewer, evidence and analysis actions, and each step is recorded as a replayable trace.

Viewer Control

Operate 3D Slicer and QuPath through predefined actions: series selection, scrolling, windowing, panning, zooming, fusion, and viewport capture.

Evidence Capture

Export key slices, RAS (right, anterior, superior) coordinates, whole-slide coordinates, masks, measurements, bookmarks, and state snapshots for deterministic scoring.

Advanced Operations

Provide vetted segmentation, registration, resampling, quantitative analysis and MONAI/VISTA3D workflows as auditable operations that agents can call.

Traces

Replayable traces connect viewer actions, software state, evidence, and final answers.

Each MedOpenClaw run records what the agent saw, which operations it called, which outputs it produced, and which evidence was available when it submitted the final answer.
