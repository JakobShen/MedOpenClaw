Computer-Use Agents in Medicine: Breaking the Barrier

We are exploring AI agents that operate medical software such as 3D Slicer, QuPath, and EHR systems, much like a human user. They can open data, use tools or models, adjust views and parameters, and inspect results to decide what to do next.

To develop and benchmark these agents, we are collecting real clinical and research workflows—especially specialized, variable, or infrequent tasks that still require manual work and lack a dedicated automated solution.

What kinds of tasks are we looking for?

  • Time-consuming or repetitiveTasks that require many manual interactions.
  • Not well solved todayTasks that existing models or standard pipelines do not handle well.
  • Multi-stepWorkflows involving segmentation, measurement, visualization, comparison, quality control, or interpretation.
  • Across tools or dataTasks that may involve multiple software tools, modalities, or time points.

Share a workflow you encounter in practice. Below, describe its goal, tools, main steps, and the evidence needed to check the result.
